# AI Trading Research Library

A curated archive for trading prompts, strategy research, backtesting, and automation, with special focus on Forex, XAUUSD (gold), scalping, entries and exits, strategy development, and automation.

## Research standards

- Treat all strategies as unverified until independently tested.
- Keep source links and licence details; write original summaries and prompts rather than copying entire third-party collections.
- Include spread, commission, slippage, position sizing, drawdown, and out-of-sample results in strategy evaluations.
- AI confidence scores are not probabilities unless statistically calibrated.
- Define entries, invalidation, stops, exits, sizing, exposure limits, and no-trade conditions.
- Start automation with backtesting and paper trading; require independent safeguards before any live execution.

## Library structure

- `01-SOURCE-CATALOG/` — repositories, papers, tools, and prompt collections.
- `02-FOREX/` — FX market structure, macro drivers, and strategies.
- `03-XAUUSD/` — gold volatility, sessions, macro drivers, and execution.
- `04-STRATEGIES/` — scalping, trend-following, mean-reversion, and price action.
- `05-TRADE-ANALYSIS/` — entry, exit, invalidation, and multi-timeframe templates.
- `06-BACKTESTING/` — test plans, metrics, and validation reports.
- `07-AUTOMATION/` — architecture, paper trading, broker/API research, and controls.
- `08-CUSTOM-PROMPTS/` — original prompts developed for this library.
- `09-EXPERIMENT-LOG/` — hypotheses, experiments, results, and decisions.

See [the source catalog](../01-SOURCE-CATALOG/README.md) for initial research leads.
